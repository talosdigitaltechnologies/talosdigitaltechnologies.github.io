About Orna

Orna is a wardrobe app by Talos Digital Technologies LLC. It helps you get dressed from the clothes you already own, plan what to wear, and find the pieces that complete your style. It is for adults (18+) in the United States, except Illinois.

  • Your closet. Photograph your pieces (up to 50 at a time) or add them from your store orders; Orna fills in the details. Track what you wear, laundry and repairs, lent-out pieces, returns and cost per wear, and see your closet stats.
  • Outfits and planning. Build outfits from your closet or arrange them on a canvas, get a daily pick and Dress me suggestions for the weather, plan looks on a calendar, and pack for trips with capsules and a packing list.
  • Discover. Browse a library of styled looks by style, occasion, season and color, save your favorites, build them from your own closet, or see them on a model.
  • AI Stylist. Chat with a Stylist that knows your closet, your plans and the weather, and get outfits made from what you own.
  • AI looks. Generate outfit images in dozens of styles, try on clothes, and with Premium see every look on your own likeness.
  • Shopping. Shop the pieces of a look at major stores, keep a wishlist with price-drop checks, and share it with a link. Orna may earn a commission from some links.
  • Make it yours. A style quiz, color seasons, challenges and badges, monthly recaps, themes and a home screen widget on Android.

Free: the full closet, outfits, calendar, trips, wishlist and Discover, 5 welcome AI looks and 10 Stylist messages a week.

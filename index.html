About Orna

Orna is a wardrobe app by Talos Digital Technologies LLC. It helps you get dressed from the clothes you already own, plan what to wear, and find the pieces that complete your style.

  • Your closet. Photograph your pieces or add them from your store orders, then track what you wear, laundry and care, and cost per wear.
  • Outfits and planning. Build outfits from your closet, plan them on a calendar, pack for trips and dress for the weather.
  • AI styling. Generate outfit ideas in dozens of styles and get feedback from the Stylist. With Premium, see looks on your own likeness.
  • Shopping. Shop the pieces of a look at major stores. Orna may earn a commission from some links.

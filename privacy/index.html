SUMMARY

This is a plain-language summary. The full policy below controls.

  • Orna is operated by Talos Digital Technologies LLC, a Florida company.
  • You must be 18 or older. Before an account is created we ask for your birth month and year; if you are under 18, we don't create an account and don't keep what you entered. Orna is available in the United States only, excluding Illinois.
  • You upload photographs of yourself and your clothing. We store them and use them as visual reference inputs to an AI model that generates outfit images for you.
  • We do not perform facial recognition. We do not create face scans, face geometry maps, or biometric templates, and we do not use your photos to identify you.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your content to train AI models.
  • When you delete an image or your account, we delete the stored file too, not just the record. Section 7 explains what is deleted and the few records we keep. Section 4.9 explains how long we keep likeness photos.
  • Shopping links are affiliate links: Orna may earn a commission from purchases through them. As an Amazon Associate, Orna earns from qualifying purchases.
  • Orna Premium is a subscription sold through Apple or Google; we never see your card details. When the app shows ads, they come from Google AdMob: free users may see them, and Premium users only if they choose to watch ads for extra generations; with your permission (and, where required, your consent) they can be personalized.
  • Anything you post to the community feed is public.

If an account was already created through Google or Apple when you enter an age under 18, we delete that account and everything in it right away. If we learn a user is under 18, we will terminate the account and delete their records. Parents or guardians who believe a person under 18 has given us information should contact admin@talosdigitaltech.com.

Location check at sign-up. When you create an account, we check the approximate U.S. state your internet connection comes from, using your IP address. If it is Illinois, we don't create the account. We don't store your IP address or the result.

3. WHAT WE COLLECT

Account information. Email address, name, and the identity provider you sign in with (Google or Apple). Passwords, where used, are stored only as a cryptographic hash by our platform provider.

Sign in with Apple. If you sign in with Apple, we store a token that Apple issues for your account. We use it only to tell Apple to disconnect Orna from your Apple ID when you delete your account, and we delete it at that time. It is never shared or used for anything else.

Birth month and year. Asked before your account is created. We use it only to confirm you are 18 or older and to work out your age for styling. It cannot be changed in the app afterwards.

Profile and styling information. Gender, age, skin tone, body size, body measurements (optional), style preferences, bio, avatar, and display preferences. If you take the optional style quiz, we save your answers (styles and colors you like or avoid, fit, your typical week, budget tier and favorite stores). If you take the color season quiz, we save only the resulting season, not your answers to its questions. The chest, waist, hips, inseam and shoe size you save in your Size book are visible only to you and are never sent to our AI provider.

Model features (optional). If you choose features for your generated model (for example East Asian or Black / African), we use them only to describe your model's face and hair when we create an outfit image without your likeness. Because this choice can reveal racial or ethnic origin, it is optional, off unless you choose it, and never used for anything else: not for ads, recommendations or taste learning. Choosing "Let Orna decide" deletes it right away, and it is deleted with your account.

What Orna learns about your taste. To make suggestions fit you, we save your thumbs up and down (with the reason, if you give one), outfits you ask not to see again, which stores you tap, and which outfit-generator settings you pick. With your favorites and what you log as worn, these decide the order of outfits, pieces and stores you are shown. They are not sent to our AI provider and are not used for advertising. You can see and reset them anytime on the Your Style page.

Photographs you upload.

  • Likeness photographs (a face photo and a body photo)
  • Clothing photographs for your digital closet
  • Photographs submitted for AI styling analysis
  • Photographs used for virtual try-on

Information our AI generates from your photos. Short text descriptions of your face and build, plus a skin tone label and color value. For likeness photos, also a short list of the clothing you are wearing in them (for example, "black crew-neck t-shirt"). Clothing attributes such as item type, colors, fit, and style category. Generated outfit images and styling analyses.

Content you create. Saved outfits, community feed posts, captions, likes, comments, shopping list items, wishlist items (including the links and pictures you add), the stores you star and basics you hide, capsules, trips you plan (destination, dates, activities and packing list), challenges you join (and purchases you mark as gifts or replacements), the layout of your Home screen, collage pictures you arrange from your closet photos, the status and notes you give your pieces, such as a repair note, support messages, and reports you submit about other users.

Reports about AI results. If you use "Report this image" or "Report this result", we save the reason you choose, any note you add (up to 500 characters), the kind of result (outfit, Try-On or Stylist) and a short description of it, such as the style or outfit name, or for the Stylist the occasion and a short summary. We never save your prompt or a photo of you with a report. A reported image is removed from your saved looks right away.

Items you import from stores. If you use Import from stores, Orna opens the store's own website inside the app and you sign in there. Orna never sees, stores or fills in your store password. On the store's order-history page only, Orna reads the order list you are looking at (item name, picture, price, order date, and size or color when shown) and shows it to you on your phone. Only the items you choose to add are saved to your Orna account, as closet items. The rest of the list is discarded when you close the import and is never sent to us. To keep the feature working we record that a page was read (which store, whether items were found, and whether you use iPhone or Android). This record has no account identifier and no order details.

A copy on your phone. So you can look at your closet, outfits, calendar and trips without a connection, the app keeps a copy of them, small copies of their pictures and your profile on your phone. It never leaves your phone and is deleted when you sign out or delete your account.

Usage and consent records. Generation and credit counts, records of AI requests (which feature was used and its estimated cost), onboarding and tutorial progress, in-app disclaimer acknowledgments, and the date and version of the Terms and of the likeness consent you accepted. If your account is reported, we also store moderation flags and administrative notes.

Payment information. Subscriptions are bought through Apple or Google, which process the payment. We never receive your card, bank or billing details. We receive your subscription status (product, active or not, purchase and renewal dates) from RevenueCat, linked to your account.

Advertising. Some versions of the Orna app show ads from Google AdMob. When the app shows ads, the Google Mobile Ads software in the app collects your IP address (for approximate location), a device or advertising identifier, the ads you see and how you interact with them, and performance and crash data, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. On iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt; in the EU and UK only with your consent. You can change this anytime in your device settings. Premium users see no forced ads. A version of the app that shows no ads does not start the Google Mobile Ads software and collects none of this.

One welcome allowance per device. The 5 welcome credits are given once per device. On iPhone we use Apple's DeviceCheck service for this: Apple lets us store two yes/no values for your device, and we use one of them to note that this device has already received its welcome credits. We never receive a device identifier, and we store only whether your account passed this check. The value stays with Apple when you delete the app or your Orna account, so a new account on the same iPhone does not receive new welcome credits.

Weather and location (optional). Weather is off unless you turn it on. You can enter a ZIP code or tap "Use my location". If you use your location, Orna asks your phone for your approximate position once, at that moment, and rounds it to about a mile before it leaves your phone. Your ZIP code or that rounded point is saved on your phone only, not in your Orna account, and is removed when you turn weather off or sign out. We keep no location history. To get a forecast, the rounded point is sent to our server, which asks the U.S. National Weather Service. Our server keeps recent forecasts by area, not by person.

What we do not collect. The Orna app does not collect your precise location, does not follow your location in the background, and contains no analytics or attribution software of its own. Our hosting platform may log standard technical information such as IP addresses and error logs in the ordinary course of running the servers.

Device biometric unlock. If you use Face ID, Touch ID, or a fingerprint to open the app, that is handled entirely by your device. Your fingerprint or face data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. We store only a setting indicating the feature is on.

4. PHOTOGRAPHS AND LIKENESS

This is the most important section. Please read it, along with Section 7.

4.1 What happens to a photo you upload

  1. The image is uploaded to our platform provider's file storage and linked to your account.
  2. Automated safety screening examines it before it can be used (see Section 4.6).
  3. An AI model examines it and returns short text descriptions (face, build, skin tone), written in words only and never as measurements or coordinates. For likeness photos, it also lists the clothing visible in the photo.
  4. When you generate an image, your stored photo is passed to an AI image model as a visual reference, together with a text prompt.
  5. The model returns a new synthetic image, which is saved if you save it.

Your likeness photos are stored and reused so you don't have to upload them each time. The text descriptions and clothing list are stored with your likeness and included in the prompt when you generate an image. The clothing list is used only to tell the image model not to copy the outfit you wore in your photo.

4.2 What we do not do

  • We do not perform facial recognition. We do not use your photo to identify you or match you against any database.
  • We do not create or store face geometry scans, facial recognition templates, faceprints, facial embeddings, facial landmark coordinates, iris or retina scans, voiceprints, fingerprints, or hand geometry scans.
  • We do not create or store body measurement vectors, body models, or segmentation masks.
  • We do not sell, lease, trade, or profit from your photographs or anything derived from them.
  • We do not use your photographs or content to train, fine-tune, or improve any AI model.

The only things we derive from your photo are the short text descriptions and the clothing list described above.

4.3 Third-party AI processing

Safety screening, photo analysis, styling analysis, and image generation are performed by Google, through the Gemini API (see Section 6). We send Google's models the prompt we build, the images the feature needs (for example your likeness photos, closet photos, or a photo you submitted), and — for outfit generation and the stylist feature — a short text summary of your profile that may include gender, age, skin tone, body size, the model features you choose, if any, and a summary of your closet. We do not send your name, email, account identifier, IP address, or any device identifier to those models.

We use Google's paid Gemini API service. Under Google's terms for that service, Google does not use your prompts, images, or generated results to improve its products or train its models; it keeps them for a limited period only to detect and prevent abuse and for legally required disclosures. We do not authorize our platform provider or any model provider to use your content for model training.

4.4 Your consent

Before you first upload a photograph of a person, we show you a separate notice explaining what happens to that image and ask for your consent. That consent is separate from accepting our Terms. Before you set up your likeness, we ask for your explicit written consent to collect, store and use your face and body photos to generate outfit images for you, including sending them to our AI provider (Section 4.3), and for you to confirm the photos are of you and that you are 18 or older. You give it by ticking the confirmation box and tapping "I Agree". We record the date and version of the consent you gave, and we retain that record so we can demonstrate it was given.

You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your likeness images in the app, which removes them from your account, deletes the stored photo files and the text descriptions and clothing list derived from them, and stops any future use of them in generation.

4.5 Photographs of other people

You may upload a photograph of another adult only with their permission. You may never upload a photograph of anyone under 18.

If you believe your image was uploaded without your permission, contact admin@talosdigitaltech.com and we will investigate and remove it from the Service.

Where we become aware of apparent child sexual abuse material, we will report it to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children as required by 18 U.S.C. § 2258A and preserve the associated records as the law requires.

4.6 Automated safety screening

Photographs of people are automatically screened before they can be used. Screening includes checks for apparent minors, explicit or suggestive content, and photographs containing more than one person. Screening fails closed — if the system is uncertain, the image is blocked rather than allowed. Blocked images are rejected and not used for generation.

4.7 Privacy setting for Stylist and Try-On photos

Orna offers a privacy setting, on by default, for photographs you submit to the Stylist and to Try-On. When it is on, the photo file is deleted right after it has been analyzed or used to create your try-on image, it is not attached to saved looks, and it cannot be shared to the community feed. A photo that is also used elsewhere in your account, such as your likeness or a closet item, is not deleted by this setting.

A person photo you add for Try-On is checked before it is used. If it does not pass, the photo is deleted right away, whatever this setting is.

When the setting is off, Stylist photos can be attached to your saved looks, and these photos are kept until you delete the saved look they are attached to or your account (see Section 7).

4.8 Illinois

The Service is not offered in Illinois. Regardless, we do not collect, capture, purchase, receive through trade, or otherwise obtain biometric identifiers or biometric information as defined by the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, 740 ILCS 14/1 et seq., for the reasons set out in Section 4.2, and we do not sell or profit from any such information. We check the approximate state at sign-up and don't create accounts from Illinois.

4.9 Likeness photos and face data: retention and destruction

What we keep. Your likeness photos (one face photo and one full-body photo); the short text descriptions, skin tone label and color value, and clothing list derived from them; and the record of your likeness consent (date and version). We never create or keep face geometry, faceprints, facial templates, or facial embeddings (Section 4.2).

Why. Only to generate outfit images for you when you ask for them.

How long, and how we destroy it.

  • A photo that is blocked by safety screening, or that can't be used, is deleted immediately and never becomes part of your likeness.
  • When you replace a likeness photo, the old photo file and the descriptions derived from it are deleted right away.
  • Your likeness is kept only while it is set up and your account is open. When you delete your likeness, or your account, we permanently delete the photo files and everything derived from them right away.
  • If you do not use Orna for 3 years (no sign-in and no use of the app while signed in), we automatically delete your likeness photos and everything derived from them.
  • Copies that our AI provider keeps to detect abuse are kept only for the limited period described in Section 4.3. Database backups are rotated out as described in Section 7.3.

What remains. Only your likeness consent record (date and version), kept as described in Section 7.5. Outfit images you generated and saved are your content and stay until you delete them.

7. DELETION AND RETENTION

7.1 What deleting does

When you delete a closet item or a saved look, it moves to Recently Deleted for 30 days so you can restore it; after that, or right away if you choose Delete forever, we delete the database record and its stored image file. When you delete an image, a post, your likeness, or your entire account, we delete the database records and the stored image files right away. Calendar plans, events, and wear history are deleted right away when you delete them, and all of it is deleted when you delete your account. The content disappears from Orna, can no longer be retrieved through the app, stops being used for any generation, and is no longer associated with your account. When you report an AI image, it is removed from your looks right away. We keep the image file only so our team can review the report, and we delete it when the review is closed, or with your account if you delete your account first.

If the same image is used by more than one of your items (for example, a saved look and a post), the file is kept until the last item using it is deleted.

7.2 How stored images are protected

  • Photographs of people, likeness photographs, closet photos, outfit photos, and generated images are held in private, access-controlled storage. Each can only be viewed through a signed link that expires.
  • Profile pictures are stored at an unlisted public address so they can be displayed. They are deleted when you delete your account.

7.3 Backups

Our platform provider keeps backups of the database for disaster recovery. Deleted information can remain in those backups until they are rotated out on the provider's schedule. We do not restore deleted personal information from backups except to recover from a system failure.

7.4 How to reduce what is stored

You can limit what you upload, use the privacy setting for Stylist and Try-On photos described in Section 4.7, and avoid posting to the public feed. You can also decline to create a likeness, and use the Service without one.

7.5 What we retain deliberately

  • Consent records (the date and version of the Terms and likeness consent you accepted) are retained after account deletion so that we can demonstrate consent if a dispute arises.
  • Support messages are kept for 2 years.
  • Moderation reports, including reports you make about AI results, are kept for 3 years and survive account deletion (without your account attached), for safety and legal reasons.
  • Moderation flags and administrative notes on your account are deleted when you delete your account, and otherwise 3 years after they were last changed.
  • Backups are described in Section 7.3.

7.6 Everything else

All other records are kept only while your account is active, and are deleted when you delete the relevant content or your account.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

You may access, correct, export, and delete your information, withdraw consent to photo processing, and opt out of any marketing email.

In the app: update your profile in Settings; delete individual images, closet items, saved looks, and posts; export your data; delete your account under Settings → Delete Account.

By email: contact admin@talosdigitaltech.com from your account email address.

We will acknowledge requests within 10 business days and respond within 45 days.

We will never charge you differently or give you a lesser experience for exercising a privacy right.

What deleting your account does

It removes your account record and profile information, your likeness photos and their descriptions and clothing lists, your closet items, your saved outfits and generated images, your feed posts, your shopping list, your wishlist, your capsules and trips, your challenges, your style quiz answers, color season, taste feedback and generator presets, and every image file you uploaded or generated. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Consent records, moderation records, and support correspondence are retained as described in Section 7.5.

Orna Support

Help with your account, your data and your subscription.

Contact us

Email admin@talosdigitaltech.com. Please write from the email address of your Orna account and tell us what happened, so we can find your account and help.

In the app you can also send us a message: open the menu (the three dots at the top right) and choose Feedback.

Delete your account and data

You can delete your Orna account yourself, at any time, in the app:

  1. Open Orna and sign in.
  2. Open the menu (the three dots at the top right) and choose Settings.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Permanently Delete.

This permanently deletes your account, your profile, your closet, saved outfits, wishlist, photos and likeness, and the image files behind them. It cannot be undone.

If you can't open the app, email admin@talosdigitaltech.com from your account's email address and ask us to delete your account.

A few records are kept after an account is deleted, with your account detached from them. The Privacy Policy, section 7, explains what is deleted, what is kept, and for how long.

A subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account or the app. Cancel it in the store first (see below).

Delete some of your data

You can delete single pieces of your data in the Orna app without deleting your account. Orna is made by Talos Digital Technologies LLC. This section explains how to delete each kind of data, what is deleted right away, and what is kept and for how long. To delete everything at once, see Delete your account and data.

Closet items

  1. Open Orna and tap Wardrobe at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Open Clothing or Accessories and tap the item.
  3. Tap the trash icon at the top of the photo, then tap Delete.

For 5 seconds you can tap Undo. After that the item moves to Recently Deleted (see below), together with its photos.

Outfits and AI looks

Every look you generate is saved to your outfits, so you delete it the same way as an outfit you made yourself.

  1. Tap Wardrobe at the bottom of the screen, then Outfits.
  2. Tap the outfit, tap the trash icon, then tap Delete.

You can also delete a look from your history: open the menu (the three dots at the top right), choose History and tap the trash icon next to the look. For 5 seconds you can tap Undo; the outfit then moves to Recently Deleted. Its picture is deleted with it: a generated image, an outfit photo you added, a collage, or a Stylist photo attached to it.

Recently Deleted: delete for good right away

Deleted closet items and outfits stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, so you can restore them if you change your mind. To delete one for good now:

  1. Open the menu (the three dots at the top right) and choose Settings, or tap Tools at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap Recently Deleted.
  3. Tap the trash icon next to the item or outfit, then tap Delete forever.

Delete forever deletes the record and its photos right away. Anything left in Recently Deleted for more than 30 days is deleted permanently, with its photos, within a day, whether or not you open Orna.

Your likeness photos

  1. Open the menu (the three dots at the top right) and choose Settings.
  2. In My Likeness, tap Delete Likeness, then tap Delete.

My Likeness is in Settings whenever you have a likeness saved, with or without Premium. Deleting it deletes both likeness photos right away, together with the descriptions, skin tone and clothing list made from them. Replacing a likeness photo deletes the old photo right away too. Looks you already generated with your likeness are not deleted; delete them as outfits (see above).

Stylist and Try-On photos

With Private Stylist & Try-On photos on (Settings, Privacy tab; it is on unless you turned it off), each photo is deleted right after it has been analyzed or used. A Try-On person photo or a Stylist photo that doesn't pass our safety check is always deleted right away. With the setting off, photos are kept. To delete them:

  1. Open the menu (the three dots at the top right), choose Settings and tap the Privacy tab.
  2. Under Delete my Stylist and Try-On photos, tap Delete photos, then tap Delete.

This deletes right away every stored Stylist and Try-On photo that isn't attached to a saved look. A Stylist photo attached to a saved look is deleted when you delete that look.

Profile picture

  1. Open the menu (the three dots at the top right) and choose Profile.
  2. Tap the pencil icon, tap Remove Photo, then tap Save.

The picture is deleted right away. When you change your profile picture, the old one is deleted right away too.

Wishlist pictures

Tap Tools, then Wishlist, tap the item and tap the trash icon. After the 5-second Undo, the item and its picture are deleted right away (wishlist items don't go to Recently Deleted). If you marked it "Bought it", the closet item keeps the picture.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Recently Deleted: closet items and outfits, for 30 days (then deleted within a day), unless you choose Delete forever.
  • Shared photos: a photo used by more than one of your items stays until the last item using it is deleted.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in our platform provider's database backups until they are rotated out on the provider's schedule. We don't restore deleted data from backups except to recover from a system failure.
  • Our AI provider: Google keeps photos and prompts sent through the Gemini API for a limited period, only to detect and prevent abuse.
  • Likeness consent: the date and version of the likeness consent you accepted is kept, also after you delete your likeness.
  • Reported AI images: if you report an AI image, we keep that image until we have reviewed your report, then delete it.

The Privacy Policy, section 7, explains deletion and retention in full. To delete your whole account and all of its data, see Delete your account and data.

Manage or cancel Orna Premium

Subscriptions are billed by the store you bought them in, so they are managed there.

App Store

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Orna Premium.
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription.

Open your App Store subscriptions

Google Play

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Orna Premium.
  4. Tap Cancel subscription.

Open your Google Play subscriptions

Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies.
